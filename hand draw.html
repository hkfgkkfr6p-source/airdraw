Privacy: hand tracking runs 100% on-device in your browser (WASM/GPU). No video frame or drawing ever leaves your machine — there is no server, no upload, no account. "Save PNG" and "Record" both write straight to your downloads folder.

How AirDraw works

  • ☝️ Hold up only your index finger to draw with your fingertip.
  • ✌️ Hold up index + middle finger to erase around that point.
  • ✊ A fist, an open hand, or no hand in frame just pauses — nothing is drawn.
  • 🤚 With 2 hands enabled, AirDraw follows one hand at a time and only switches to the other once the first leaves the frame.
  • ⏱️ Starting the camera runs a 3-2-1 countdown so you have time to get in position.
  • 🔴 Hit Record to save a video of your drawing session (with your camera and strokes) straight to your downloads.
  • ⚙️ Open Customize (top right) for brush/eraser size, colors, mirroring, camera visibility, and the hand skeleton.
  • 🔒 Everything runs locally in your browser. Stop the camera any time — it fully releases the webcam.
